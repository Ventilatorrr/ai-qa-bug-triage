# Product Vision: AI QA Bug Triage

## Vision

AI QA Bug Triage is a web-based bug triage system designed to help QA analysts track software defects throughout their lifecycle. The system provides AI-assisted suggestions for bug classification, descriptions, and reproduction steps while keeping QA analysts in control of final decisions.

## Target Users

The primary users are QA analysts who need to record, manage, track, and triage software defects.

## Problem

QA analysts need an efficient and structured way to manage bugs throughout their lifecycle. Initial bug triage can also involve repetitive work such as classifying defects and preparing clear descriptions and reproduction steps.

## Core Value

The system helps QA teams manage bugs efficiently while providing AI-assisted triage that supports, rather than replaces, human QA decision-making.

## High-Level Scope

The application will provide functionality for:

- Managing software projects
- Creating and managing bug reports
- Tracking bugs through their lifecycle
- AI-assisted bug triage
- Managing and reviewing AI suggestions
- Supporting QA testing and defect-management workflows

## Out of Scope

The project is not intended to be:

- A replacement for Jira or other full-scale project-management platforms
- A commercial SaaS product
- A production-grade enterprise bug-management platform
