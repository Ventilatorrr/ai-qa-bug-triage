Who is responsible

AI QA Bug Triage is a personal, non-commercial portfolio project for software testing. The data controller for the personal data described here is Sebastjan Vrtačnik, a private individual in Slovenia. Privacy contact: aiqabugtriage@gmail.com.

This policy is provided under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR) and the Slovenian Personal Data Protection Act (ZVOP-2). It explains what the application processes, why, on which legal basis, who receives it and for how long it is kept.

Accounts

Registration collects an email address and a password. The application stores the email address, a bcrypt hash of the password (never the password itself), an internal account ID and the creation time. Logging in issues an access token that expires after 8 hours and cannot be renewed. Email addresses are not verified; if an account has been registered with your address without your permission, contact us.

Purpose and legal basis: creating and authenticating your account and providing the service you registered for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). An email address and password are required; without them no account can be created.

Projects, members and bug reports

The application stores project names, project memberships and roles, and bug reports: titles, descriptions, environment, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, versions, classifications, assignments, lifecycle information, authorship and timestamps. All members of a project can see its reports and the email addresses and roles of its members.

A Project Owner can add any registered user to a project using their email address, without that user's prior agreement. QA Analysts and Developers can leave a project at any time; a Project Owner can remove members.

Purpose and legal basis: providing project collaboration and bug management to the users who take part (Article 6(1)(b)). Showing your email address and role to members of projects you were added to, and storing other people's personal data that users enter, relies on the legitimate interest of project members in working together on their bug reports (Article 6(1)(f)). That interest is limited to what the service needs: the application only requires member email addresses and roles.

Bug reports are not intended to contain personal data. Do not enter personal data about other people in report text, and never enter special categories of data such as health information. If a report contains personal data about you, you can ask for it to be removed or object to its processing (see Your rights).

Try Demo

Visitors can start a Demo from the Login page without registering. Each Demo creates five generated accounts with example.com email addresses and random passwords that are stored only as hashes and never shown. The visitor is signed in as the Demo's Project Owner. Each Demo has two projects: one with fictional E-Commerce bug reports and one with copies of this project's genuine development defect reports, which were reviewed for sensitive information before inclusion.

Demo access ends when the visitor logs out or the 8-hour session expires; because the generated credentials are never shown, a Demo cannot be reopened. Each Demo is deleted automatically once it is 7 days old, including its generated accounts, projects, reports, memberships, AI Assist usage records and anything the visitor added or changed. Cleanup runs hourly, so deletion normally happens within an hour after that. The number of Demos that can be started and stored is limited without using IP addresses.

Purpose and legal basis: letting visitors try the application without registering (Article 6(1)(f)). Demo accounts are not linked to a person. Do not enter personal or confidential information in a Demo.

AI Assist and OpenAI

AI Assist is optional and runs only when a Project Owner or QA Analyst explicitly requests it on the New Bug form or while editing a bug in Triage. It sends to OpenAI the current form's Title, Affected Version, Environment, Description, Steps to Reproduce, Expected Result, Actual Result, Severity, Priority and Assignee ID, including unsaved edits. It also sends up to five recently updated reports from the same project, excluding the report being edited, with those fields shortened to 1,000 characters, their internal IDs and bug numbers, and the user IDs and roles of the project's QA Analysts and Developers who can be assigned. Their email addresses are not included in this member list, but any email address or other personal information typed into a report field is sent with that text. This historical content may have been entered by other project members.

Access tokens, passwords and configuration secrets are never added to AI requests, but report text is not checked for secrets. Do not include passwords, API keys, personal information or confidential material in reports or AI requests.

Suggestions are advisory. They change only the form after you choose to use them, and only Create or Save stores values. No decision about you is made solely by automated means. To enforce daily usage limits, the application records the account and date of each request sent to OpenAI; these records are deleted after their UTC day ends, normally within an hour, or earlier with the account or Demo. The dismissible data-sharing notice is information only; dismissing it is not consent and does not change what is sent.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the triage suggestions you request (Article 6(1)(b)). Sending other members' report content and assignable members' user IDs and roles relies on the legitimate interest of project members in consistent triage and assignee suggestions (Article 6(1)(f)); this is limited to five recent reports from the same project and to members of that project. Usage records rely on the legitimate interest in preventing misuse and controlling costs (Article 6(1)(f)).

OpenAI processes AI Assist requests as a processor under its data processing addendum, which forms part of the OpenAI Services Agreement for API use. Under that addendum, customers based in the EEA contract with OpenAI Ireland Ltd., and personal data from the EEA is processed on the instructions of OpenAI Ireland Ltd. The application requests store=false. According to OpenAI's API data controls, API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default and may be kept in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, or longer where the law requires.

Hosting, logs and backups

The application is hosted by Render Services, Inc., a US company, in its Frankfurt (EU Central) region in Germany. Its database is stored on a persistent disk in that region. Render processes this data as a processor under its published data processing addendum, which supplements Render's Terms of Service.

Application logs record the request time, method, address path (which can include project and bug numbers), response status and network information such as IP addresses. They do not record form content or passwords, and are kept for 7 days. Render takes a snapshot of the database disk every 24 hours and keeps each snapshot for 7 days.

Render also processes its own service usage data, such as connection source and destination information and activity logs, as an independent controller to operate and secure its platform and prevent misuse. See Render's Privacy Policy.

Purpose and legal basis: operating, securing and restoring the service (Article 6(1)(f)).

Pages load no external fonts, scripts, analytics or advertising; all page resources are served by the application itself.

International transfers

Although the application and its database are hosted in the EU, Render is based in the United States and lists US-based subprocessors on its security page: currently Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud Platform, Cloudflare and ClickHouse. Personal data may therefore be accessed or processed outside the EU, for example for network traffic, logs, support or security. Render's data processing addendum provides that these transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Render is certified and which is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision, or otherwise on the EU standard contractual clauses.

AI Assist uses OpenAI's standard API endpoint without EU data residency, so requests are processed outside the EU, including in the United States. Under OpenAI's data processing addendum, OpenAI Ireland Ltd. transfers personal data to OpenAI affiliates or third parties outside the EEA on the basis of standard contractual clauses or a European Commission adequacy decision. You can ask for more information about these safeguards at aiqabugtriage@gmail.com.

Browser storage and cookies

The application sets no cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage for the access token, your theme, bug page-size preferences and the dismissed AI-notice preference, and sessionStorage for bug-list sorting and temporary interface messages. Only the access token is sent to the server, to authenticate your requests. These items are strictly necessary for signing in or remember choices you made, so they do not require consent.

Logging out removes the access token. Other preferences remain until you change them or clear this site's data in your browser, which also signs you out and makes the AI notice reappear.

Account deletion and retention

There is no self-service account deletion. To delete your account, email aiqabugtriage@gmail.com from your account's email address. When an account is deleted, the projects it owns are deleted with their reports and memberships, its memberships in other projects end, its assignments there are cleared and its AI Assist usage records are deleted. Reports it created in other people's projects are kept for those projects without its email address, but their text is not changed and may still contain information you entered. A placeholder record without the email address or password remains so the account ID is never reused.

Registered accounts and their projects are kept until the account is deleted on request or the service is permanently retired. Temporarily suspending the service does not delete its database. When the service is permanently retired, the application database and its persistent disk will be deleted; according to Render's documentation, each disk snapshot remains available for 7 days after it was created. Demos are kept for 7 days and AI usage records until the end of their UTC day, each plus up to about an hour. After deletion, copies can remain in database snapshots for up to 7 days, in application logs for up to 7 days, and with OpenAI for up to 30 days for requests already sent. If a snapshot is ever restored, deletions made after it are applied again. Records of privacy requests are kept in the privacy mailbox, limited to the information needed to show how each request was handled, for 12 months after the request is resolved (Article 6(1)(c)). They are kept longer only where a legal obligation or a specific dispute requires it (Article 6(1)(c) or (f)).

Version 1.0.0 started with a new database; accounts and projects from earlier versions were not carried over. The earlier version's service is suspended and its stored data has not yet been deleted. It will be retired and its stored data deleted after version 1.0.0 has been deployed and verified in production.

Security

The application uses password hashing, authenticated API requests, project membership checks and role-based permissions. Request sizes, text fields and concurrent password checks are limited to protect the service, without using IP addresses. No system can guarantee complete security; use fictional, non-sensitive content for portfolio demonstrations.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict its processing and, for data you provided under Article 6(1)(b), to receive it in a portable format. These rights have conditions and exceptions set out in the GDPR.

Right to object: where processing relies on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. Processing then stops unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms.

To make a request, email aiqabugtriage@gmail.com from your account's email address so it can be matched to your account. Requests are free of charge and answered within one month; this can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case you will be told why. Emails to this address are received and stored in a Gmail account provided by Google.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR). In Slovenia this is the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (Informacijski pooblaščenec Republike Slovenije), Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, gp.ip@ip-rs.si, www.ip-rs.si. You can also complain to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement took place.
